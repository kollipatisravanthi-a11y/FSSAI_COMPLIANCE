SAMPLE FACILITY SOP/MANUAL (FOR DEMO ONLY)

Facility: Example Foods Pvt Ltd
Document: Hygiene + Storage SOP (Extract)

1. HYGIENE & CLEANING
- Food-contact surfaces (tables, cutters, trays) are cleaned and sanitized DAILY at end of shift.
- Sanitizer concentration is prepared per label instructions and verified using test strips.
- Cleaning activities are recorded in the Cleaning Log after each task.
- Cleaning records are retained for a minimum of 12 months and are available during audits.

2. STORAGE / TEMPERATURE CONTROL
- Chiller temperature limit: 0–5°C.
- Freezer temperature limit: -18°C or colder.
- Temperatures are recorded at least twice daily (opening and closing) in the Temperature Log.
- Any out-of-limit reading triggers corrective action: isolate product, assess safety, and document the deviation.

3. PERSONNEL HYGIENE
- Staff follow documented handwashing procedure before entering the processing area and after any contamination risk.
- Hairnets and clean uniforms are mandatory inside processing areas.

4. DOCUMENTATION
- Logs maintained: Cleaning Log, Temperature Log, Pest Control Service Record, Training Record.
