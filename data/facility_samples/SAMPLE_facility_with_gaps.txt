SAMPLE FACILITY SOP/MANUAL (FOR DEMO ONLY)

Facility: Demo Snacks Unit
Document: SOP Excerpts

1. HYGIENE & CLEANING
- Food-contact surfaces are cleaned WEEKLY as needed.
- Staff decide cleaning frequency depending on workload.
- Cleaning is performed using detergent and water.
- Records may be maintained when required.

2. STORAGE / TEMPERATURE CONTROL
- Chiller temperature is monitored every shift.
- Temperature log is maintained.
- Chiller temperature should be kept safe.

3. PERSONNEL HYGIENE
- Staff wash hands sometimes.
- Protective clothing is used when required.
